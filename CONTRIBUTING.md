# Contributing

Proof Protocol is not accepting contributions at this time.

## Pull requests

Pull requests will not be reviewed or merged and will be closed automatically. Please do not submit changes to specification text, code, or documentation.

## Feedback

Questions, errata, and comments may be submitted as GitHub issues. By submitting an issue, you agree that:

- Your feedback is provided voluntarily and without restriction.
- The maintainer may use, adapt, or disregard it at their sole discretion.
- Submitting feedback does not make you an author, contributor, or rights holder in any specification or code in this organization.
- No obligation of compensation, attribution, or confidentiality arises from your submission.

Do not submit confidential, proprietary, or patented material in an issue.

## Citation

To reference a specification, use the citation in its repository's `CITATION.cff` or the "Cite as" section of its README.
